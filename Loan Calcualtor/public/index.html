Know what’s behind your estimate.

01

Repayments, made clear

We amortise principal and interest monthly, cap the final payment at the balance owing, and compare the full schedules with and without extras.

02

A starting point for eligibility

Home loan capacity uses your monthly surplus at the entered rate plus a 3 percentage point buffer, capped by your assumed LVR. Commercial capacity uses your selected cash-flow coverage and buffer.

03

Honest about accuracy

95% bank assessment accuracy has not been established. Lenders also assess credit history, employment, expenses, valuations and product conditions. Published rates are not personal offers; fixed rates may expire before the loan term.
